## Description (4000 characters max)

Giant Buddy connects to your Giant e-bike with RideControl+ over Bluetooth and turns your iPhone into a full ride computer, navigator, and bike-health dashboard.

LIVE RIDE DASHBOARD
See everything at a glance while you ride: speed, cadence, torque, motor power, estimated rider power, battery level, remaining range, distance, and time. The dashboard includes a live map showing your position and recorded trail, current weather conditions, and heart rate from your Apple Watch. A compact landscape layout keeps key metrics visible when your phone is mounted on the handlebars.

ROUTES AND NAVIGATION
Import GPX routes, create new routes directly on the map, or search for a destination by address or place name. Browse point-of-interest shortcuts for cafés, bike shops, charging stations, and other common ride stops. Save favorite places for quick reuse. Before you start, preview each route with distance, elevation profile, estimated time, and a battery-range prediction based on your past rides.

During navigation, Giant Buddy shows remaining distance, completion progress, and your position on the route. If you go off-route, it detects the deviation and reroutes automatically. Optional voice prompts and haptic feedback keep you informed without looking at the screen.

RIDE RECORDING AND HISTORY
Record GPS tracks alongside full bike telemetry in the background — even with your phone in your pocket. When auto-record is on, recording starts as soon as the bike connects, whether the app is open or not; a notification confirms the recording began. Browse your ride history with summary cards and route maps. Open any ride to explore interactive charts for speed, motor power, rider power, elevation, and battery over time. Scrub through the timeline to inspect any moment of the ride.

Export rides as CSV for data analysis or GPX for Strava and Komoot. Upload directly to Strava from the app. Save any recorded ride back as a reusable route for next time. Open GPX files from other apps — tap a .gpx file in Files, Safari, email, or AirDrop and Giant Buddy imports it as a new route.

ANALYTICS
See your riding trends across a week, month, year, or all time. Track total distance, ride count, average speed, elevation gain, battery consumption, efficiency, and personal records.

BIKE INFO AND MAINTENANCE
View your bike's frame number, odometer, firmware versions, and protocol status data. Monitor battery charge, health percentage, capacity, and charge cycles over time. Set up maintenance reminders tied to odometer distance or calendar dates, and review the bike's error-log history.

SAFETY
Enable crash detection during rides. If a crash is detected, Giant Buddy shows a full-screen countdown alert. Dismiss it if you are okay; otherwise it automatically sends your GPS location via SMS to your emergency contacts. Test the alert from Settings without sending any messages.

APPLE WATCH AND WIDGETS
The watchOS companion app gives you at-a-glance ride data on your wrist and shares heart-rate readings back to the iPhone during recording. Add a Home Screen widget showing your most recent ride summary, or follow an active ride on the Dynamic Island and Lock Screen with Live Activities.

WORKS WITH YOUR GIANT E-BIKE
Giant Buddy communicates using the Giant GEV Bluetooth protocol. It has been tested with the Stormguard E+ 2 (2023) and works with Giant e-bikes equipped with RideControl+ BLE telemetry. On supported bike and display combinations, you can also toggle the light and adjust the assist level from the app.

Requires iOS 17.0 or later. Apple Watch features require watchOS 10 or later.
