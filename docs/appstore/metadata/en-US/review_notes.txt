Giant Buddy is an independent ride computer for Giant e-bikes with RideControl+ Bluetooth. Attached: GiantBuddy-App-Review.mp4 (screen recording), ride_2026-10-09.gpx and ride_2026-10-09.csv (sample files from that ride).

1. SCREEN RECORDING
Recorded on an iPhone 18 Pro Max running iOS 27, connected to a real e-bike (Giant Stormguard E+ 2, 2023). It starts on the Home Screen, launches the app and shows the main flow: connecting to the bike, the live ride dashboard and automatic recording, A-to-B turn-by-turn navigation, ride history with charts, export and Strava upload, saved routes, place search, bike status and a crash detection test. The app has no account creation, login, account deletion, user-generated content or in-app purchases.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
For owners of Giant e-bikes with RideControl+. The bike's own display shows little and keeps no history. Giant Buddy turns iPhone and Apple Watch into a ride computer: live speed, motor and rider power, cadence, torque and battery; automatic ride recording even without getting the phone out of your pocket; cycling navigation with a battery prediction for the route; ride analysis and export; and bike health (odometer, battery, maintenance).

3. SETUP
- No account or login; no credentials are needed.
- Live data needs a compatible Giant e-bike: Bike tab > Scan for Bikes, then tap the bike. Recording starts automatically when connected (Settings > Auto-Record on Connect).
- Without a bike:
  - Ride tab > magnifier: search a place or category (e.g. Bicycle Repair) and tap Navigate.
  - My Routes > + > Import GPX: import the attached ride_2026-10-09.gpx (save it to Files first), or Create Route.
  - History, Settings and the Apple Watch app.
- Crash detection test: Settings > Emergency Contacts & Crash Detection > Test Crash Alert. It shows the countdown only and sends nothing.
- Strava upload is optional and uses Strava's own OAuth sign-in (any Strava account).

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Apple MapKit: maps, place search, directions.
- Apple WeatherKit: current weather and forecast.
- Apple CloudKit: syncs rides and routes to the user's private iCloud database.
- Apple HealthKit (optional): saves cycling workouts while excluding the energy the e-bike has put into the effort.
- WatchConnectivity: iPhone to Apple Watch app.
- Open-Meteo Elevation API (api.open-meteo.com): elevation for coordinates; no account, no personal identifiers.
- Strava API (optional): OAuth sign-in and upload of rides the user chooses.
- Messages: if a crash countdown isn't dismissed, the app opens the Messages composer prefilled with the location; the user must tap Send. Nothing is sent automatically.
- Bluetooth LE: only to the user's own e-bike.
There is no developer server, analytics, advertising or tracking.

5. REGIONS
The app works the same in all regions. Map, search, directions and weather coverage follow Apple's services in each country.
